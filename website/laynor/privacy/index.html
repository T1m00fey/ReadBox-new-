Laynor does not continuously record what you type. Text is sent for AI processing only when you deliberately choose an AI action. We do not sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy explains how Timofey Aleksandrovich Yudin, an individual developer operating under the ReadBox name (“ReadBox,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with the Laynor mobile application, its custom keyboard extension, the Laynor pages at readbox.online, and related support and feedback services (together, the “Service”).

For applicable data-protection laws, ReadBox is the controller of personal data described in this Policy. By using Laynor, you acknowledge this Policy. Where consent is legally required, we ask for it separately.

2. Data we process

Text submitted for an AI action

When you tap an action such as Rewrite, Fix, or Custom Command, Laynor sends the selected text—or, if no text is selected, a limited portion of the text immediately before the cursor—together with the selected action and any custom instruction to our backend for processing. The generated result is returned to your device.

Normal typing is not transmitted to ReadBox. Laynor does not continuously log keystrokes and does not maintain a message-history database.

Feedback and support

If you send feedback from the app, we collect the category you choose, the message you write, your optional contact information, app language, app version, platform, submission status, and submission time. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

Technical data

Our infrastructure providers may automatically process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure network requests, such as IP address, request time, service identifiers, and diagnostic or security logs. Laynor does not use advertising identifiers and does not currently include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics.

Device permissions and local settings

Keyboard preferences, such as haptic-feedback settings and the selected keyboard language, are stored locally on your device or in the app group shared by the Laynor app and keyboard extension.

iOS “Full Access” is required only for features that need a network connection, including AI actions. Core typing remains available without using an AI action. Laynor does not record or store microphone audio. If you use Apple’s dictation features, they are controlled by iOS and Apple’s applicable terms and privacy notices.

Data we do not intentionally collect

Laynor does not require an account and does not intentionally collect your contacts, photos, precise location, payment-card details, or microphone recordings.

3. How and why we use data

We process information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • perform the AI edit or custom command you request;
  • return the generated text and operate the Service;
  • receive, review, and respond to feedback or support requests;
  • maintain security, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and protect the Service;
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our Terms of Use.

Depending on your location, our legal bases include performance of our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing Laynor, your consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations.

4. Service providers and transfers

We do not sell or rent personal data. We disclose data only as needed to operate the Service or comply with law:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide the backend, Cloud Functions, and Firestore storage used for feedback. Their systems may process network and service data necessary to deliver those services.
  • OpenAI processes text, the selected action, custom instructions, and generated output when you request an AI action. According to OpenAI’s API data controls, API content is not used to train OpenAI models by default. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs containing prompts and responses for up to 30 days unless different approved data controls or legal requirements apply.
  • Apple distributes the app and provides iOS keyboard and optional dictation functionality under Apple’s own terms and privacy notices.
  • Authorities or professional advisers may receive information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, or protect rights, safety, and security.

We require service providers that process personal data for us to protect it consistently with this Policy and applicable law, and to use it only to provide their contracted services. Providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, transfers are supported by recognized legal safeguards.

5. Retention and security

AI requests: ReadBox does not intentionally store the text or generated response in a message-history database after the request is completed. Temporary infrastructure and security logs may be retained by our service providers as described above, including OpenAI abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default.

Feedback: Feedback, optional contact details, and related metadata are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to review the submission, respond to you, improve Laynor, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We then delete or anonymize the information when it is no longer needed.

Support email: Support correspondence may be retained while your request is open and afterward where reasonably necessary for follow-up, security, recordkeeping, or legal compliance.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including encrypted network connections and access controls. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your privacy choices and rights

You control when text is submitted: do not tap an AI action if you do not want that text processed. You can revoke Full Access in iOS Settings or remove the Laynor keyboard at any time. Doing so disables network-dependent keyboard features.

Subject to local law, you may ask us to access, correct, delete, or restrict our use of your personal data; object to certain processing; obtain a portable copy where applicable; or withdraw consent. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Because Laynor does not require an account and AI requests are not stored by ReadBox as message history, we may not be able to identify or retrieve a past AI request. For feedback or support data, include the contact information used in your submission so we can locate it.

To make a privacy request, email support@ireadbox.ru. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request.

7. Children

Laynor is not directed to children under 13, or a higher minimum age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the applicable minimum age. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as Laynor changes or legal requirements evolve. We will post the revised version here and update the date above. If a change materially affects your rights, we will provide additional notice where required.
